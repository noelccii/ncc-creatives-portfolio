My Story

I am Noel Christopher P. Crausus II, living in Ozamiz City, Misamis Occidental. I'm currently an Animation and Multimedia Arts student, expressing my ideas through art and various forms of digital media.

I've always been interested in learning new creative skills since I was a kid, always looking for new ways to venture further and express myself, while trying to understand what my purpose really is and how best to show it.

Outside of academics, I enjoy working out and finding ways to improve myself while inspiring the people around me. I'm also a freelance digital artist pursuing independence and personal growth through my work.

Interests

  • Film & Video Production
  • Photography
  • Fitness & Working Out
  • Entrepreneurship & Financial Growth

Goals

My goal as a creative is to find real purpose in what I do, and eventually reach a point where I can do the things I love without financial worry.

  1. Explore different interests to discover what truly excites me
  2. Build knowledge and experience in what I care about most
  3. Turn that passion into something valuable I can offer others
  4. Reach independence while still doing meaningful work
